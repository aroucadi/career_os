## 2. Evaluation Mandate (7-Step Execution Protocol)

Execute your analysis following this exact sequence:

1. **Synthesize 4-Pillar Scores with Evidence**:
   - Pillar 1: Change Management & AI Adoption (Max 30)
   - Pillar 2: Agentic AI & Automation (Max 25)
   - Pillar 3: Scaled Agile & Delivery (Max 25)
   - Pillar 4: Executive Alignment & Commercial Strategy (Max 20)
   - Provide concrete evidence citations from both the JD and the resume for each pillar score.

2. **Identify Strongest Matches and Material Gaps**:
   - Explicitly highlight the top 3-4 structural overlaps.
   - List technical, organizational, or domain gaps (ATS keywords, tool stack, industry).

3. **Separate Hard Dealbreakers from Negotiable/Compensable Gaps**:
   - Distinguish non-negotiable structural blockers from gaps that can be neutralized by adjacent competencies.

4. **Evaluate Mandatory Constraints**:
   - Location & Working Model (e.g. On-site vs Hybrid vs 100% Remote).
   - Languages (Candidate's fluent languages vs any mandatory languages specified in JD).
   - Seniority & Scope (Strategic / Lead / Architecture vs misaligned anti-roles).
   - Contract / Compensation alignment (Freelance Daily Rate vs Permanent).

5. **Strategic Verdict & Decision Hierarchy**:
   - Output an unambiguous decision: **GO**, **CONDITIONAL GO**, or **NO-GO / PIVOT**.
   - **MANDATORY NEGATIVE CONSTRAINT**: Do not infer a GO solely from a high technical fit if a mandatory constraint (location, language, working model) is unmet.

6. **Assess Negotiation Leverage & Market Rate**:
   - Estimate fair market pricing: Freelance Daily Rate (TJM in EUR) and Permanent Base Salary aligned with candidate's commercial targets.
   - State whether the candidate holds high, medium, or low leverage.

7. **Draft Targeted Pitch ({{PITCH_TYPE}})**:
   - Maximize interview conversion probability by addressing the employer's unstated fears.
   - Strictly anchor the pitch in the candidate's verified proof metrics and telemetry provided above.
   - If verdict is NO-GO / PIVOT, formulate a high-leverage counter-proposal pitch instead of a standard application.
