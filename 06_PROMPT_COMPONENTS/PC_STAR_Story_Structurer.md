# PC: STAR Story Structurer Subroutine

## Purpose
Subroutine for taking raw candidate anecdotes and formatting them into high-converting STAR interview responses.

---

## The STAR Formatting Constraint

- **Situation (15% of response)**: Establish company scale, business context, and core challenge in 2 sentences.
- **Task (15% of response)**: Explicitly state the candidate's personal responsibility.
- **Action (50% of response)**: Detail 3-4 specific technical or leadership steps taken by the candidate.
- **Result (20% of response)**: State the quantified metric improvement and team learning.

---

## Structural Response Template

```markdown
• **Situation**: While at [Company], our team faced [Challenge/Problem], impacting [Metric/Scale].
• **Task**: As [Role Title], my objective was to [Specific Goal/Ownership].
• **Action**: First, I [Action 1]. Second, I engineered [Action 2]. Finally, I collaborated with [Team] to [Action 3].
• **Result**: As a result, we [Metric Improvement, e.g., reduced downtime by 40%], saving $[Amount] annually.
```
