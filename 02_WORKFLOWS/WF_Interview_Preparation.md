# WF: Interview Preparation & Simulation Workflow

## Purpose
Prepares candidates for high-stakes interviews by predicting targeted questions, structuring STAR stories, and reframing resume weaknesses.

---

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Question Prediction Engine**: Analyze target Job Description to predict:
   - *5 Likely Behavioral Questions*.
   - *5 Technical / Domain Deep-Dive Questions*.
   - *3 Tough / Weakness Questions* (e.g., employment gaps, lack of direct tool experience).
2. **STAR Story Structuring**: Use `06_PROMPT_COMPONENTS/PC_STAR_Story_Structurer.md` to format candidate stories into Situation (15%), Task (15%), Action (50%), and Result (20%).
3. **Response Evaluation**: Audit candidate practice answers against `04_RUBRICS/RUBRIC_STAR_Interview_Scorer.md`.
4. **Questions for the Interviewer**: Formulate 5 high-leverage reverse interview questions establishing candidate strategic depth.
