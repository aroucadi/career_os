# WF: Recruiter Cold Outreach Strategy Workflow

## Purpose
Execution framework for cold outreach targeting agency recruiters, in-house talent acquisition leads, and hiring managers.

---

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Target Persona Identification**: Determine outreach recipient (Agency Recruiter vs. In-House Corporate Recruiter vs. Direct Hiring Manager).
2. **Hook Formulation**: Isolate 1 primary candidate achievement metric matching the hiring manager's immediate business need.
3. **Message Generation**: Deploy `05_TEMPLATES/OUTREACH/TPL_Recruiter_Outreach_Messages.md` to draft:
   - *InMail Hook (<100 words)*.
   - *Direct Email Hook (<150 words)*.
4. **Follow-Up Cadence**: Establish 3-step follow-up schedule (Day 1: Initial outreach, Day 4: Quick value add, Day 8: Final polite check-in).
