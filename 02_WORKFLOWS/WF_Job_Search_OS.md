# WF: Job Search Operating System (30/60/90 Outbound Campaign)

## Purpose
Builds a structured 30/60/90-day job search campaign blueprint, weekly outreach cadences, and tracking metrics.

---

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Target Account List (TAL) Setup**: Guide candidate to identify 30 target companies across Tier 1 (Dream), Tier 2 (Target), and Tier 3 (Safety).
2. **Channel Strategy Allocation**:
   - *Cold Outbound (Recruiter InMail)*: 40% of effort.
   - *Warm Referral Networking*: 40% of effort.
   - *Direct ATS Applications*: 20% of effort.
3. **Weekly KPI Dashboard Setup**: Establish target metrics:
   - 15 Outbound Contacts / Week.
   - 5 Warm Intro Requests / Week.
   - 10 Tailored Applications / Week.
4. **Campaign Execution Deliverables**: Deploy `05_TEMPLATES/JOB_SEARCH_OS/*` templates to track applications.
