# WF: Executive Branding & Strategic Positioning Workflow

## Purpose
High-stakes positioning pipeline for Senior Director, VP, C-Suite, and Board candidates.

---

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Executive Value Proposition Audit**: Isolate candidate P&L scale, organizational leadership footprint, and track record of commercial transformation.
2. **Executive Portfolio Generation**:
   - *Executive Resume*: Deploy `05_TEMPLATES/RESUME/TPL_Resume_Executive.md`.
   - *Executive Biography*: Deploy `05_TEMPLATES/OUTREACH/TPL_Executive_Bio.md`.
   - *Thought Leadership / Board One-Pager*.
3. **Board & Investor Positioning**: Audit messaging against investor, board, and executive recruiter expectations using `04_RUBRICS/RUBRIC_Executive_Scorecard.md`.
