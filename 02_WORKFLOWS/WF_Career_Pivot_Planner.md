# WF: Career Pivot Planner Workflow

## Purpose
Formulates 5 distinct, realistic career transition paths for candidates switching industries or functional roles.

---

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Viability Check**: Pass target field and candidate CV into `01_DECISION_TREES/DT_Pivot_Viability.md`.
2. **5-Path Generation**: Formulate 5 transition paths ranging from immediate low-friction moves to long-term reach pivots:
   - *Path 1: Direct Transferable Move* (Same industry, new function).
   - *Path 2: Industry Shift* (Same function, new industry).
   - *Path 3: Hybrid Bridging Role* (Stepping-stone position).
   - *Path 4: Upskilling-Led Pivot* (Requires certification/portfolio).
   - *Path 5: High-Upside Non-Traditional Route* (Startup / consulting pivot).
3. **Path Specification**: For each path, produce:
   - Current Strengths & Transferable Experience.
   - Skill Gaps & 90-Day Learning Roadmap.
   - Suggested Portfolio Projects.
   - Expected Salary Range & Timeline (3–12 months).
   - Probability of Success Score (%).
