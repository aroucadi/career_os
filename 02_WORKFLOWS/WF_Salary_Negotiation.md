# WF: Salary & Compensation Negotiation Workflow

## Purpose
Strategic playbook for evaluating offers, benchmarking market compensation, and formulating risk-free counter-offers.

---

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Offer Breakdown & Value Audit**: Deconstruct offer into Base, Annual Bonus, Equity, Signing Bonus, and Benefits using `03_KNOWLEDGE_BASE/COMPENSATION/KNOW_Total_Comp_Structure.md`.
2. **Market Benchmarking**: Query `03_KNOWLEDGE_BASE/COMPENSATION/KNOW_Salary_Benchmarking.md` to establish 50th, 75th, and 90th percentile compensation for role level and geography.
3. **Leverage Assessment**: Determine negotiation leverage based on competing offers, candidate specialized skill urgency, and current interview pipeline stage.
4. **Counter-Offer Generation**: Deploy `03_KNOWLEDGE_BASE/COMPENSATION/KNOW_Negotiation_Tactics.md` to draft a polite, collaborative counter-offer message targeting specific salary or signing bonus bumps.
