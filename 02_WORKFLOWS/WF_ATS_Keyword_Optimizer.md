# WF: ATS Keyword Optimizer Workflow

## Purpose
Extracts, categorizes, and naturally embeds required hard keywords into candidate documents to maximize ATS search ranking.

---

## Step-by-Step Execution Protocol

1. **Extraction & Frequency Analysis**: Analyze target JD to extract top 20 hard skills, tool acronyms, and industry terms.
2. **Gap Audit**: Compare extracted JD keywords against candidate CV text.
3. **Keyword Priority Classification**:
   - *Critical Keywords (High Frequency in JD & Required)*.
   - *Secondary Keywords (Preferred Skills)*.
   - *Domain Terminology & Acronyms*.
4. **Natural Insertion Mapping**: Provide explicit bullet-by-bullet integration guidance to insert missing terms without artificial stuffing.
