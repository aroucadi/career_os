## About the Role
Salt is partnering with a leading organisation in Abu Dhabi to appoint a **Head of AI Adoption**.
This senior role will lead the organisation-wide adoption of approved AI tools and AI-enabled ways of working. The focus is not simply on training or promoting AI awareness. You will be responsible for driving genuine behavioural change, sustained usage and measurable business value.

## Key Responsibilities
- Develop and lead the enterprise AI adoption strategy and roadmap.
- Create role-based adoption plans for leaders, managers and employees.
- Translate AI capabilities into practical, repeatable workplace use cases.
- Establish clear adoption targets, KPIs and reporting.
- Use data and employee feedback to identify barriers and improve adoption.
- Build and manage an internal network of AI champions.
- Coordinate training, communications, demonstrations and engagement programmes.
- Partner with Product, Engineering, Data, Security and Transformation teams.
- Measure the impact of AI on productivity, service delivery and ways of working.
- Ensure employees understand how to use approved AI tools responsibly.

## Requirements & Qualifications
- 8+ years of experience in enterprise adoption, digital transformation, change, product adoption or a related field.
- Proven experience leading a large-scale adoption or behavioural-change programme.
- Strong understanding of generative AI and enterprise AI assistants.
- Hands-on familiarity with platforms such as ChatGPT Enterprise, Microsoft Copilot, Claude or similar.
- Able to translate technology into clear, practical business use cases.
- Strong analytical skills, including experience using adoption and usage data.
- Excellent stakeholder-management, communication and facilitation skills.
- Comfortable working directly with senior executives.
- Experience leading AI adoption across a large workforce, particularly within a government, regulated or complex enterprise environment, would be highly advantageous.
