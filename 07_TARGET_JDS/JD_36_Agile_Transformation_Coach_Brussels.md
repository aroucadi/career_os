# Agile Transformation Coach — EITS Brussels

- **Role**: Agile Transformation Coach
- **Client / Target Organization**: EITS (Enterprise IT Solutions), Brussels, Belgium
- **Location**: Brussels, VLG Belgium (Hybrid)
- **Contract Type**: 13 months with possible extension (Contract / Freelance)
- **Work mode**: Hybrid
- **Languages**: English, Dutch OR French

---

## Job Description & Context
The assignment of the Agile Transformation Coach consists of 6 major strategic and operational deliverables across EITS (Enterprise IT Services) and AWOW (Agile Way of Working):

### 1. Agile Maturity Assessment & Organisatieanalyse
**Responsibilities:**
- Conducting maturity assessments within AWOW and relevant transformation domains within EITS.
- Analyzing the current way of working, IT governance, collaboration with architecture, security, portfolio management, leadership, and agile maturity, among others.
- Identifying strengths, gaps, risks, and priority areas for improvement.
- Challenging the current Agile Way of Working based on external expertise, best practices, and proven agile principles.

**Expected Results / Deliverables:**
- Agile Maturity Assessment report for the IT department.
- Overview of identified strengths, gaps, and risks.
- Prioritized areas for improvement, recommendations, and action plan with concrete proposals for the further evolution of AWOW and of the Strategic/Tactical layer of EITS.

### 2. Transformatiestrategie & Roadmap
**Responsibilities:**
- Translating digital strategy into concrete IT transformation initiatives, including concrete objectives, priorities, and initiatives.
- Determining the desired future situation together with management, Agile Coaches, and relevant stakeholders.
- Assisting with making strategic choices and prioritization within the transformation.
- Identifying dependencies, risks, and preconditions for successful implementation.

**Expected Results / Deliverables:**
- Supported and prioritized transformation roadmap for AWOW and the broader EITS transformation.
- Clear transformation objectives and milestones.
- Overview of dependencies, risks, and necessary decisions.
- Concrete priorities for the short, medium, and long term.

### 3. Operating Model & Agile Governance
**Responsibilities:**
- Assisting in the design of a future-oriented operating model for EITS.
- Advising on roles, responsibilities, decision-making structures, and collaboration models.
- Translating agile principles into workable IT governance within the context of EITS and IT.
- Monitoring the coherence between teams, management, portfolio, governance, and strategic objectives.

**Expected Results / Deliverables:**
- A clear and widely supported target operating model for the future operation of EITS.
- Concrete Agile governance framework.
- Clear roles, responsibilities, and decision rights.
- Recommendations for the implementation and sustainable embedding of the operating model.

### 4. KPIs, Metrics & Succescriteria
**Responsibilities:**
- Defining IT-specific KPIs, metrics, and maturity indicators for AWOW and the broader transformation, for example, delivery, flow, and quality metrics.
- Determining how progress, maturity, impact, and value can be monitored in a transparent manner.
- IT Management and Agile Coaches assist in interpreting and using metrics for continuous improvement.

**Expected Results / Deliverables:**
- Concrete OKR/KPI and metrics framework.
- Clear success criteria and maturity indicators.
- Proposal for structural follow-up and reporting.
- Measurable indicators that can demonstrate the progress and impact of the transformation.

### 5. Coaching & Mentoring of the Agile Coaching Team
**Responsibilities:**
- Coaching and mentoring of the Agile Coaching Team.
- Strengthening competencies related to organizational change, stakeholder management, management coaching, change management, systems thinking, and agile transformation.
- Assisting Agile Coaches with complex coaching and transformation issues.
- Challenging the team and introducing new methodologies, insights, and best practices.
- Further developing the autonomy of the Agile Coaching Team.

**Expected Results / Deliverables:**
- Strengthened Agile Coaching Team with increased maturity and autonomy.
- Concrete development of coaching and transformation competencies within the team, starting from the team's skill matrix.
- Practical coaching and intervention techniques for complex situations.
- Structured knowledge transfer, so that expertise remains sustainably present within the coaching team.

### 6. Management Coaching & Agile Leadership
**Responsibilities:**
- Guiding and challenging IT management and executives on agile leadership.
- Supporting the evolution from traditional management and governance patterns to leadership that supports autonomy, transparency, and continuous improvement.
- Supporting management with complex choices and tensions within the transformation.

**Expected Results / Deliverables:**
- Conducting workshops with management on Agile leadership with the aim of increasing insight into their role within the (digital) transformation.
- Clear leadership principles and desired behaviors to support AWOW.
- Improved alignment between IT management, Agile Coaching Team, and IT teams.
