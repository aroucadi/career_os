# IT AI Delivery Senior Manager (m/f) — International Retailer (Annecy) / The Recruiter

## Company Overview
International retailer based in Annecy, France. Undergoing a major AI transformation and looking for an **IT AI Delivery Senior Manager** to lead the delivery and operationalisation of its AI strategy across IT.

Reports directly to the **IT Senior Director Digital Technologies**. Opportunity to build a new AI organization from the ground up (greenfield), initially managing external partners/consultants before establishing internal teams.

## Key Responsibilities

### 1. AI Delivery & Platform Ownership
- Own AI delivery and the AI platform from implementation through production.
- Ensure reliability, scalability, cost control (FinOps/Token economics), and compliance.

### 2. Team & Organization Building
- Build and develop internal teams (AI Platform and AI Enablement organizations).
- Manage external partners, vendors, and program budgets.

### 3. AI Adoption & Enablement
- Drive AI adoption across IT through hands-on delivery, training, and collaboration.
- Set high delivery standards around AI quality, evaluation, observability, safety, and performance.
- Partner with senior leadership to translate AI strategy into effective technology delivery.

## Profile & Requirements
- **Proven Experience**: Leading engineering, platform, or technology delivery teams.
- **Production Ownership**: Responsibility for availability, costs, and operational performance.
- **AI Credentials**: Strong knowledge of AI technologies and AI-native delivery (Agentic AI, LLMOps, ML infrastructure).
- **Change Management**: Experience driving tech adoption and organizational change.
- **Vendor & Budget Management**: Strong stakeholder, vendor, and budget control.
- **Languages**: Fluent English required; French is a strong asset.
