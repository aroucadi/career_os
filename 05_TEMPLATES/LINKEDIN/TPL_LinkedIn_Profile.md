# TPL: LinkedIn Profile Copy Layout Blueprint

## Purpose
Pre-formatted layout schema for LinkedIn Headline, About section, Experience, and Skills sections.

---

## 1. Headline Variations

- **Option A (Metric Hook)**: `[Target Title]` | Ex-`[Company]` | Scaled `[System/Product]` from `[X]` to `[Y]` | `[Top 3 Hard Skills]`
- **Option B (Domain Authority)**: `[Target Title]` helping `[Target Audience]` achieve `[Business Outcome]` | Specializing in `[Skill 1, Skill 2, Skill 3]`

---

## 2. About Section Blueprint

```markdown
👋 Hi, I'm [Name] — a [Target Title] specializing in [Core Domain].

🚀 WHAT I DO:
I help [Target Customer/Company Type] build, scale, and optimize [Product/System/Process].

💡 CAREER HIGHLIGHTS:
• [Achievement 1 with quantified metric].
• [Achievement 2 with scale/revenue impact].
• [Achievement 3 with leadership/award context].

🛠 TECHNICAL & STRATEGIC STACK:
• Core: [Skill 1, Skill 2, Skill 3]
• Tools: [Tool 1, Tool 2, Tool 3]

📫 CONNECT WITH ME:
Feel free to reach out via InMail or email at [Email Address].
```
