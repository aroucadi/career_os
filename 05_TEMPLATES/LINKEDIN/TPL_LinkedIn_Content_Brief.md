# TPL: LinkedIn Featured Content & Banner Brief

## Purpose
Visual brief and text templates for LinkedIn profile banner design, featured posts, and thought leadership positioning.

---

## 1. Banner Design Concept Brief
- **Visual Theme**: Minimalist dark-mode or clean corporate aesthetic matching domain industry (e.g., code snippets for tech, metrics chart for finance/ops).
- **Text Overlay**: `[Target Value Proposition]` (e.g., *"Building Resilient AI Infrastructure"*).

---

## 2. Featured Section Content Matrix
- **Slot 1 (Top Portfolio Item)**: Link to GitHub repository, technical whitepaper, or live app.
- **Slot 2 (Case Study / Post)**: High-performing post detailing a complex technical challenge solved.
- **Slot 3 (Resume PDF)**: Clean, single-page downloadable executive CV.
