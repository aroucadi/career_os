# TPL: 30/60/90-Day Outbound Job Search Campaign Plan

## Purpose
Strategic roadmap for organizing outbound job search activities across 30-day milestone phases.

---

## Phase 1: Days 1–30 (Foundation & Target Account Setup)
- **Goal**: Complete document tailoring, optimize public profiles, and launch initial outreach.
- **Key Actions**:
  - Rebuild resume using `WF_Resume_Rewriter.md`.
  - Optimize LinkedIn profile using `WF_LinkedIn_Optimizer.md`.
  - Build Target Account List (TAL) of 30 target companies.
  - Send initial 20 outbound recruiter InMails.

---

## Phase 2: Days 31–60 (Active Outreach & Pipeline Acceleration)
- **Goal**: Convert outbound leads into phone screens and hiring manager interviews.
- **Key Actions**:
  - Maintain 15 outbound messages per week.
  - Conduct 5 informational networking calls weekly.
  - Prepare STAR interview stories using `WF_Interview_Preparation.md`.
  - Follow up on all pending active applications.

---

## Phase 3: Days 61–90 (Interview Execution & Offer Negotiation)
- **Goal**: Finalize interview loops, benchmark compensation, and execute negotiation.
- **Key Actions**:
  - Complete panel interviews and technical assessments.
  - Evaluate incoming offers using `WF_Salary_Negotiation.md`.
  - Finalize written offer acceptance and onboarding transition.
