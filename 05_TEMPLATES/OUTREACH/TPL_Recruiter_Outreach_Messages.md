# TPL: Recruiter Outbound InMail & Cold Email Templates

## Purpose
High-converting cold messaging templates tailored for agency recruiters, internal talent acquisition leads, and hiring managers.

---

## 1. LinkedIn InMail Script (<100 Words)

> **Subject**: [Target Role Title] — Candidate Inquiry (`[Primary Metric Hook]`)
>
> Hi [Recruiter First Name],
>
> I saw that [Company Name] is hiring a **[Target Role Title]**. Over the past [X] years at [Previous Company], I’ve specialized in [Core Skill], recently [Key Metric Accomplishment, e.g., scaling infrastructure to 50k req/sec / growing regional ARR 40%].
>
> My background aligns closely with your search criteria. I’d love to connect and share my CV if you’re still reviewing candidates for this role.
>
> Best,  
> **[Your Name]**

---

## 2. Cold Email to Hiring Manager (<150 Words)

> **Subject**: Quick question regarding [Team Name] at [Company Name]
>
> Hi [Manager First Name],
>
> I’ve been following [Company Name]’s recent expansion in [Domain/Product]. 
>
> As a [Target Role Title], I specialize in [Core Hard Skill]. In my previous role at [Previous Company], I [Action Verb + Quantified Metric Achievement]. 
>
> Given your team's focus on [Specific Problem from Job Description], I wanted to share my resume in case you are looking for support on [Project/Initiative].
>
> Do you have 5 minutes for a quick chat next Tuesday?
>
> Best regards,  
> **[Your Name]**  
> [LinkedIn Link] | [Portfolio Link]
