# TPL: One-Page Executive Biography Blueprint

## Purpose
Format layout for senior leadership executive biographies used in board applications, media appearances, and executive search packets.

---

## Layout Schema

```markdown
# [EXECUTIVE FULL NAME]
[Current Executive Title, e.g., Chief Technology Officer / VP of Global Operations]

---

## BIOGRAPHY OVERVIEW
[Candidate Name] is an executive leader with over [X] years of experience driving [P&L Scale / Turnaround / Global Expansion] across [Industry 1] and [Industry 2]. Most recently serving as [Current Title] at [Company Name], [LastName] oversees a [Metric Scope, e.g., $100M P&L and 300-person global team], orchestrating [Key Strategic Transformation].

---

## CAREER ACCOMPLISHMENTS & VALUE CREATION
• **Enterprise Scaling & Growth**: Scaled revenue from $[X]M to $[Y]M across [Timeframe], resulting in [Exit / IPO / Market Leadership].
• **Operational Transformation**: Restructured international engineering operations, cutting OPEX by [X]% while increasing delivery velocity.
• **Board & Capital Markets**: Participated in [M&A / Series C Fundraising / Board Governance], presenting quarterly strategic updates to institutional investors.

---

## BOARD POSITIONS & AFFILIATIONS
- **Board Director** | [Company / Non-Profit Name], [Year] – Present
- **Member** | Executive Leadership Network, [Year] – Present

---

## EDUCATION
- **Master of Business Administration (MBA)** | [Business School], [Year]
```
