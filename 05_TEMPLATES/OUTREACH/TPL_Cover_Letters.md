# TPL: Modern Direct Cover Letter Templates

## Purpose
High-converting 3-paragraph cover letter templates designed to eliminate corporate fluff and highlight immediate candidate ROI.

---

## 3-Paragraph High-Impact Structure

```markdown
Dear [Hiring Manager Name / Hiring Team],

I am writing to express my strong interest in the **[Target Role Title]** position at **[Company Name]**. With over [X] years of experience in [Core Domain], specializing in [Key Hard Skill 1] and [Key Hard Skill 2], I have built my career delivering [Primary Metric Hook, e.g., high-throughput distributed systems / $10M+ SaaS growth].

At **[Current/Previous Company]**, I [Action Verb + Primary Achievement with Metric]. This experience directly matches your team's requirement for someone who can [Key Challenge Mentioned in Job Description]. Additionally, my expertise in [Skill 2] allows me to [Second Impact Point].

I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my background in [Core Domain] can support [Company Name]'s upcoming objectives for [Target Initiative/Product]. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,  
**[Candidate Name]**  
[Phone Number] | [Email Address] | [LinkedIn URL]
```
