# TPL: Networking & Informational Interview Scripts

## Purpose
Conversational scripts for securing warm referrals, requesting informational interviews, and re-engaging past professional connections.

---

## 1. Warm Intro / Alumni Network Request

> Hi [Name],
>
> I saw that you graduated from [University Name] / previously worked at [Company Name]! I’m currently exploring opportunities in [Target Domain] and have been following [Target Company]'s work on [Project/Product].
>
> I would love to ask you 2-3 quick questions about your experience with the team. Would you be open to a brief 10-minute virtual coffee next week?
>
> Best,  
> **[Your Name]**

---

## 2. Re-activating Past Manager / Colleague Connection

> Hi [Name],
>
> Hope you're doing well! It's been a while since we worked together on [Past Project] at [Past Company]. 
>
> I'm currently looking at making my next move into a **[Target Role Title]** position. Given your perspective in [Industry], I’d love to get your thoughts on how the market is shaping up. 
>
> Let me know if you have time for a quick catch-up call sometime soon!
>
> Best,  
> **[Your Name]**
