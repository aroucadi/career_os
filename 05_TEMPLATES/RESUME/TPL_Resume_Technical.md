# TPL: Technical Resume Layout Blueprint (Engineering, Data, AI)

## Purpose
Pre-formatted, single-column ATS-compliant Markdown layout tailored for Software Engineers, Data Engineers, AI/ML Engineers, and DevOps.

---

## Layout Schema

```markdown
# [CANDIDATE FULL NAME]
[City, State/Country] | [Phone Number] | [Professional Email] | [LinkedIn URL] | [GitHub/Portfolio URL]

---

## PROFESSIONAL SUMMARY
[2-3 sentence overview targeting role title, years of experience, core technical stack, and primary metric achievement.]

---

## TECHNICAL SKILLS
- **Languages**: [Python, Java, TypeScript, SQL, Go, C++]
- **Frameworks & Libraries**: [React, Next.js, Node.js, PyTorch, TensorFlow, Spring Boot]
- **Cloud & DevOps**: [AWS, GCP, Docker, Kubernetes, Terraform, CI/CD, Datadog]
- **Databases & Tools**: [PostgreSQL, Snowflake, Redis, Elasticsearch, Git, Jira]

---

## PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE

### [COMPANY NAME] — [City, Country]
**[Target Role Title]** | [Month YYYY] – Present
- [Action Verb] [quantified technical accomplishment using XYZ formula].
- [Action Verb] [system architecture, scalability, or performance improvement metric].
- [Action Verb] [cross-functional collaboration or engineering process optimization].

### [PREVIOUS COMPANY NAME] — [City, Country]
**[Previous Role Title]** | [Month YYYY] – [Month YYYY]
- [Action Verb] [quantified technical achievement].
- [Action Verb] [bug reduction, latency improvement, or infrastructure cost savings].

---

## EDUCATION & CERTIFICATIONS
- **[Degree Name] in [Field]** | [University Name], [Year]
- **[Certification Name]** (e.g., AWS Certified Solutions Architect) | [Issuing Org], [Year]
```
