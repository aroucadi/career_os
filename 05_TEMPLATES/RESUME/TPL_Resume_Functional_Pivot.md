# TPL: Functional / Hybrid Resume Layout Blueprint (Career Pivot)

## Purpose
Hybrid resume layout emphasizing transferable competencies, portfolio projects, and skill cross-walks for career changers.

---

## Layout Schema

```markdown
# [CANDIDATE FULL NAME]
[City, State/Country] | [Phone Number] | [Professional Email] | [LinkedIn URL] | [Portfolio URL]

---

## CAREER TRANSITION SUMMARY
[Statement connecting past background to target field, highlighting transferable core competencies and target commitment.]

---

## TRANSFERABLE CORE COMPETENCIES
- **[Target Competency 1, e.g., Data Analysis & Modeling]**: [Brief statement of capability + evidence].
- **[Target Competency 2, e.g., Product Strategy & Roadmap]**: [Brief statement of capability + evidence].
- **[Target Competency 3, e.g., Cross-Functional Leadership]**: [Brief statement of capability + evidence].

---

## CAPSTONE & PORTFOLIO PROJECTS
### [Project Title / Capstone Name] | [Link to Live Project / GitHub]
- [Description of problem solved, tools used, and quantitative result].
- [Specific technical or functional contribution].

---

## PROFESSIONAL EXPERIENCE (RE-FRAMED)

### [COMPANY NAME] — [City, Country]
**[Past Role Title]** | [Month YYYY] – Present
- [Bullet re-framed to emphasize transferable competency].
- [Bullet highlighting leadership, project delivery, or analytical rigour].

---

## EDUCATION & UPSKILLING
- **[Certification / Bootcamp Name]** | [Provider Name], [Year]
- **[Degree Name]** | [University Name], [Year]
```
