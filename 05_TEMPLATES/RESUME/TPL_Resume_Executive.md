# TPL: Executive Resume Layout Blueprint (Director, VP, C-Suite)

## Purpose
High-impact, executive-level layout focusing on commercial leadership, P&L scale, organizational transformation, and board governance.

---

## Layout Schema

```markdown
# [CANDIDATE FULL NAME]
[City, State/Country] | [Phone Number] | [Professional Email] | [LinkedIn Profile URL]

---

[P&L-focused executive statement establishing commercial scale, organizational leadership scope ($M P&L, global team size), and track record of enterprise value creation.]

- **Commercial Transformation**: Enterprise Revenue Expansion, OPEX Optimization, Organizational Restructuring
- **Governance & Public Relations**: Investor Relations, Regulatory Compliance, ESG, Media Representation

---

## EXECUTIVE EXPERIENCE & COMMERCIAL LEADERSHIP

### [ENTERPRISE / COMPANY NAME] — [City, Country]
**[Chief Executive Officer / VP of Engineering / General Manager]** | [Month YYYY] – Present
- **P&L & Fiscal Impact**: [Directed $XXM P&L, scaling ARR from $XXM to $XXM across X quarters].
- **Organizational Architecture**: [Led XX-person global organization across X international hubs].
- **Strategic M&A / Growth**: [Orchestrated acquisition/integration of $XXM asset, expanding market share by XX%].

## BOARD & ADVISORY ROLES
```
