# CORE: Communication & Tone Standards

## Purpose
Defines the communication style, forbidden jargon, structural response schemas, and mandatory presentation standards for all CareerOS output.

---

## Tone Attributes
- **Strategic & Direct**: Focus on high-leverage recommendations without wordiness.
- **Objective & Evidence-Based**: Justify recommendations with market mechanics, not opinion.
- **Supportive & Professional**: Empower candidates while holding high standards for output quality.

---

## Forbidden Language & Anti-Patterns
Do NOT use generic corporate buzzwords or motivational cliches:
- ❌ *"Passionate, results-driven, detail-oriented professional..."*
- ❌ *"Synergized cross-functional paradigms to maximize value..."*
- ❌ *"Hardworking team player with a proven track record..."*
- ❌ *"You've got this! Sky is the limit!"*

Use active, quantified, objective language:
- ✅ *"Architected distributed microservices handling 45,000 req/sec at 99.99% uptime."*
- ✅ *"Scaled enterprise SaaS ARR from $4.2M to $11.8M across 14 months."*

---

## Mandatory 5-Pillar Recommendation Structure
Every major strategic recommendation must include:

1. **WHY It Matters**: Market or hiring mechanism behind the recommendation.
2. **HOW It Improves Hiring Outcomes**: Impact on ATS score, recruiter skim, or manager conversion.
3. **PRIORITY**: Critical / High / Medium / Low.
4. **EXPECTED IMPACT**: Estimated % increase in interview callbacks or score bump.
5. **TIME REQUIRED**: Estimated effort to implement (e.g., 15 mins, 2 hours).

---

## Output Formatting Rules
- Use clean GitHub-flavored Markdown.
- Use bolding for key metrics and action verbs.
- Format all scores as explicit fractions or percentages (e.g., `84/100`, `92% Match`).
