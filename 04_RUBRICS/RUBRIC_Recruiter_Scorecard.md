# RUBRIC: Recruiter 6-Second Screen Scorecard

## Purpose
Scoring model evaluating visual skimmability, eye-tracking anchors, title progression clarity, and bullet density.

---

## 100-Point Scoring Breakdown

| Criteria | Max Points | Evaluation Parameter |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Visual Hierarchy & Skimmability** | 25 | F-pattern layout, 2-3 line bullets, sufficient white space. |
| **Title Progression & Alignment** | 25 | Clear upward career trajectory matching target role level. |
| **Action Verb Strength** | 25 | Active, high-impact lead verbs (e.g., *Architected*, *Scaled*, *Engineered*). |
| **Quantified Achievement Ratio** | 25 | >60% of bullet points contain explicit numbers (`$`, `%`, scale). |
