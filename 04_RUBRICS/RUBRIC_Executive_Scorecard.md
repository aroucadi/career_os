# RUBRIC: Executive & Leadership Presence Scorecard

## Purpose
Evaluation rubric measuring P&L scale, organizational leadership scope, commercial impact, and executive branding authority.

---

## 100-Point Scoring Breakdown

| Criteria | Max Points | Evaluation Parameter |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **P&L & Fiscal Ownership** | 30 | Clear articulation of budget scope, revenue ownership, or cost optimization scale. |
| **Organizational Scale** | 25 | Reporting hierarchy depth, team headcounts, global hub leadership. |
| **Commercial Strategy & Vision** | 25 | Market expansion, M&A integration, strategic enterprise initiatives. |
| **Board / Governance Authority** | 20 | Investor relations, board reporting, external industry positioning. |
