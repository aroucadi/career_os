# RUBRIC: STAR Competency Interview Response Scorecard

## Purpose
Quantitative rubric evaluating candidate behavioral interview answers against STAR criteria.

---

## 100-Point Response Breakdown

| STAR Phase | Target % Weight | Evaluation Criteria |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Situation (S)** | 15% | Clear baseline context, business challenge, and scale established quickly. |
| **Task (T)** | 15% | Explicit candidate ownership responsibility articulated. |
| **Action (A)** | 50% | Detailed step-by-step technical and strategic execution explained. |
| **Result (R)** | 20% | Quantified business outcome (`$`, `%`, speed, quality metric) delivered. |
