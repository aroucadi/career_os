# RUBRIC: Career Pivot Feasibility Scorecard

## Purpose
Evaluates candidate transition feasibility across skill overlap, industry barriers, and learning curve duration.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

1. **Skill Overlap Score (0–40 Points)**: Ratio of direct and transferable competencies to target role requirements.
2. **Industry Accessibility Index (0–30 Points)**: Target domain openness to non-traditional candidates.
3. **Retraining Gap Score (0–30 Points)**: Time and capital required to achieve baseline target qualifications.
