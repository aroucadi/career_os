# RUBRIC: ATS Technical Compatibility Scorecard

## Purpose
100-point rubric assessing raw document parseability, structural hierarchy, section header standard compliance, and keyword density.

---

## 100-Point Scoring Breakdown

| Criteria | Max Points | Evaluation Parameter |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Document Architecture** | 30 | Single-column layout, no tables, no text boxes, clean PDF/DOCX structure. |
| **Section Header Standards** | 20 | Standardized headings (`Experience`, `Education`, `Skills`). |
| **Keyword Match Ratio** | 30 | Presence of core target hard skills and technical acronyms. |
| **Contact / Header Hygiene** | 10 | Contact info in body, clean location details, standard email/phone. |
| **Date & Chronology Format** | 10 | Consistent `MM/YYYY` or `Month YYYY` date formatting without overlaps. |
