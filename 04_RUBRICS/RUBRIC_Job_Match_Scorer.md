# RUBRIC: Job Description Match & Gap Scoring Model

## Purpose
Quantitative framework comparing candidate experience against specific job postings to calculate Match Fit % and Interview Probability.

---

## Weight Distribution

$$\text{Total Match Score} = 0.50(\text{Hard Technical Requirements}) + 0.30(\text{YOE & Domain Alignment}) + 0.20(\text{Education & Certifications})$$

---

## Match Bands & Interview Likelihood

- **85–100% Match**: High Qualification. High interview callback probability (>70%).
- **70–84% Match**: Moderate Qualification. Acceptable for direct application with tailored CV (35–69% interview probability).
- **50–69% Match**: Low/Gap Qualification. Requires strategic referral networking or functional positioning (15–34% probability).
- **<50% Match**: Severe Qualification Gap. Direct application not recommended without bridging projects.
