# KNOW: Industry Intelligence — Operations & Delivery

## Purpose
Taxonomy, standards, and resume evaluation for Operations, Supply Chain, Logistics, and Service Delivery roles.

---

## Core Domain Taxonomy & Focus Areas

- **Operations Management**: Process Improvement, Lean Six Sigma, Process Mapping, Resource Allocation, Capacity Planning.
- **Supply Chain & Logistics**: Inventory Optimization, Vendor Procurement, Warehouse Management, Freight Logistics.
- **Service Delivery**: SLA Management, Quality Assurance, Escalation Management, Operational Excellence.

---

## High-Impact Target Metrics
- SLA Compliance Rate (`%`).
- Operational Expense (OPEX) Reduction (`$`, `%`).
- Cycle Time / Fulfillment Reduction (`Hours`, `Days`).
- Inventory Turn Rate Improvement.
