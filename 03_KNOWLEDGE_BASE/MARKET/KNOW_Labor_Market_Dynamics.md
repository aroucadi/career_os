# KNOW: Macro Labor Market Dynamics & Talent Economics

## Purpose
Provides macroeconomic context on hiring cycles, candidate supply vs. demand balance, and employer vs. candidate leverage shifts.

---

## Hiring Market Cycles & Positioning Strategy

1. **Expansionary / Candidate Market (High Liquidity)**
   - *Characteristics*: Aggressive hiring, multiple competing offers, rapid wage growth, flexible credential requirements.
   - *Strategy*: Aggressive salary positioning, fast-track promotions, applying for reach roles.

2. **Contractionary / Employer Market (Low Liquidity)**
   - *Characteristics*: Layoffs, high applicant volume per opening, stringent job requirements, delayed hiring decisions.
   - *Strategy*: High precision keyword targeting, warm referral networking, highlighting risk mitigation and immediate ROI.

3. **Domain-Specific Disruption (Structural Shift)**
   - *Characteristics*: Contraction in legacy roles alongside explosion in specialized domains (e.g., AI/ML, Cybersecurity).
   - *Strategy*: Rapid skill translation and positioning toward high-demand growth vectors.
