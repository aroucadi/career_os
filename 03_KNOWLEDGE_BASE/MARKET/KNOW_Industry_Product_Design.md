# KNOW: Industry Intelligence — Product & UX Design

## Purpose
Taxonomy, metrics, and resume evaluation standards for Product Management, Technical Program Management (TPM), and UX/UI Design.

---

## Core Domain Taxonomy & Focus Areas

- **Product Management**: Roadmap Ownership, User Story Mapping, PRD Authoring, Product-Led Growth (PLG), A/B Testing, Feature Prioritization (RICE framework).
- **UX/UI Design**: Design Systems, Figma, User Research, Usability Testing, Wireframing, Interaction Design.
- **Technical Program Management**: Cross-functional alignment, dependency tracking, sprint velocity, launch orchestration.

---

## High-Impact Target Metrics
- Monthly Active Users (MAU) / Daily Active Users (DAU) Growth.
- Activation & Retention Rates (`%`).
- Conversion Funnel Drop-off Reduction (`%`).
- Feature Adoption Rate (`%`).
- Customer Satisfaction / NPS Scores.
