# KNOW: AI Disruption Index & Job Defensibility Matrix

## Purpose
Evaluates AI automation vulnerability across job functions and provides re-positioning frameworks for high-defensibility career paths.

---

## AI Vulnerability Taxonomy

```
                          Function Vulnerability Scale
                                       │
       ┌───────────────────────────────┼───────────────────────────────┐
       ▼                               ▼                               ▼
High Risk (Automation)        Moderate Risk (Augmentation)    Low Risk (Defensible)
Routine Copywriting, Basic     Software Engineering, Data      System Architecture, High-Stakes
Data Entry, Tier-1 Support     Analysis, Product Design        Sales, Executive Leadership, Ops
```

---

## Strategic Re-Positioning Toward AI Defensibility

- **For Software Developers**: Pivot from routine CRUD frontend coding to AI Infrastructure, LLM Orchestration, System Architecture, and Security.
- **For Marketers**: Pivot from SEO copy generation to Growth Strategy, Brand Narrative, Community Building, and Data Analytics.
- **For Operations**: Pivot from manual scheduling to Process Automation, Supply Chain Resilience, and Vendor Negotiation.
