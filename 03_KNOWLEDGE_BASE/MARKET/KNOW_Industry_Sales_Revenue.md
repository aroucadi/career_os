# KNOW: Industry Intelligence — Enterprise Sales & Revenue

## Purpose
Hiring metrics, quota achievement framing, and domain taxonomy for Enterprise Account Executives, SDRs, Sales Leaders, and RevOps.

---

## Core Domain Taxonomy & Focus Areas

- **Enterprise Sales**: B2B SaaS Sales, Outbound Prospecting, MEDDPICC / SPIN Selling, Contract Negotiation, Pipeline Management.
- **Revenue Operations (RevOps)**: Salesforce / Hubspot Administration, Deal Desk, Sales Forecasting, Territory Planning.

---

## High-Impact Target Metrics
- Quota Attainment (`% of quota`, e.g., `145% of $1.2M quota`).
- New Annual Contract Value (ACV) / ARR Generated (`$`).
- Average Deal Size (`$`).
- Sales Cycle Compression (`Months` to `Weeks`).
