# KNOW: Industry Intelligence — Management Consulting & Corporate Strategy

## Purpose
Hiring standards, case interview expectations, and resume framing for Management Consulting (MBB, Big 4) and Corporate Strategy roles.

---

## Core Domain Taxonomy & Focus Areas

- **Management Consulting**: MECE Problem Structuring, Financial Modeling, Client Stakeholder Alignment, Operational Restructuring, Strategic Due Diligence.
- **Corporate Strategy**: M&A Integration, Competitive Intelligence, Market Entry Strategy, Long-Term Financial Planning.

---

## High-Impact Target Metrics
- Project Dollar Value / Scope ($M).
- Client EBITDA Improvement ($M / %).
- Cost Transformation Savings ($M).
- Market Share Expansion (%).
