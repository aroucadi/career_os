## High-Viability Skill Translation Examples

1. **Management Consultant → Product Strategy / BizOps**
   - *Translation*: Framework problem-solving and client decks translate directly into Product Requirement Documents (PRDs) and GTM Strategy.
2. **Software Engineer → Technical Product Manager**
   - *Translation*: System architecture expertise translates into managing technical backlogs, API strategy, and developer relations.
3. **Teacher / Academic → Corporate L&D / Customer Success**
   - *Translation*: Curriculum design and classroom management translate into Enterprise Onboarding, Enablement, and CS Training.
