# KNOW: Global Remote & Hybrid Hiring Intelligence

## Purpose
Provides technical and positioning knowledge for candidates seeking remote, global distributed, or hybrid roles.

---

## Key Remote Positioning Indicators

1. **Asynchronous Communication Mastery**: Demonstrating clear documentation habits, Notion/Confluence proficiency, and self-directed task management.
2. **Autonomous Execution Proof**: Evidencing past remote delivery without micro-management supervision.
3. **Cross-Timezone Collaboration**: Experience working across US, EU, or APAC business hours.

---

## Global Compensation & Tax Dynamics

- **Tiered Geographic Pay Bands**: Enterprise remote employers frequently adjust compensation based on candidate location (Tier 1: US Metro, Tier 2: Secondary Metro, Tier 3: Global Remote).
- **Employer of Record (EOR) Contracting**: Familiarity with Deel, Remote.com, and international contractor arrangements.
