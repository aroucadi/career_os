# KNOW: Hiring Manager Evaluation Framework

## Purpose
Explains how functional hiring managers evaluate technical execution depth, problem-solving methodologies, and commercial impact.

---

## Hiring Manager vs. Recruiter Mindset

Unlike recruiters who evaluate broad fit and keyword alignment, **Hiring Managers ask**:
- *"Can this candidate solve my specific team pain point starting on Day 30?"*
- *"Does this candidate understand the technical architecture and business trade-offs of their past work?"*
- *"Can they operate independently without requiring constant management supervision?"*

---

## Core Evaluation Pillars

1. **Problem-Action-Result Clarity**: Does the candidate articulate *why* a project was executed, *how* they built it, and *what* business metric improved?
2. **Domain Depth vs. Surface Claims**: Are technical tools cited alongside real implementation context (e.g., *"Built microservices with Go using gRPC"* vs. *"Proficient in Go"*)?
3. **Ownership Scope**: Did the candidate lead the initiative, architect the solution, or simply assist a larger team?
4. **Failure & Trade-off Awareness**: Is there evidence of handling scale, edge cases, system bottlenecks, or technical debt?
