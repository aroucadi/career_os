# KNOW: Executive Presence & Leadership Signaling

## Purpose
Defines signaling indicators required for Director, VP, and C-Suite candidates to convey strategic authority, board alignment, and commercial oversight.

---

## Operational vs. Executive Framing

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Operational Bullet (IC/Mid-Level)                                 │
│ "Managed software development projects using Agile sprints."     │
└─────────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────┘
                                  │
                                  ▼
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Executive Bullet (Director/VP/C-Suite)                           │
│ "Directed 45-person engineering org across 3 global hubs,        │
│ delivering $14M product portfolio on time while cutting OPEX 18%."│
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## Key Executive Signals

1. **P&L & Fiscal Responsibility**: Explicit mention of annual budget scope (e.g., *"$5M–$50M P&L management"*).
2. **Organizational Architecture**: Team size, hierarchy scale, global hub expansion, and talent retention metrics.
3. **Board & Investor Engagement**: Governance participation, fundraising support, M&A due diligence, investor reporting.
4. **Commercial & Strategic Vision**: Enterprise pivot execution, market expansion, product portfolio growth.
5. **Change Management**: Navigating enterprise digital transformations, restructuring, or post-merger integration.
