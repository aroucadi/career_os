# KNOW: Resume Red Flags & Friction Elimination

## Purpose
Identifies fatal resume errors, visual friction points, and narrative anti-patterns that trigger instant rejection by hiring teams.

---

## Top 8 Resume Red Flags

1. **Unexplained Employment Gaps (>6 Months)**: Gaps without context raise questions regarding candidate activity.
   - *Fix*: Label gap with active positioning (e.g., *"Career Sabbatical & Independent Consulting"*, *"Upskilling & Technical Certification"*).
2. **Chronic Job Hopping (<12 Months across multiple consecutive roles)**: Signals flight risk or performance issues.
   - *Fix*: Group contract/consulting roles under a single umbrella entity (e.g., *"Independent Tech Consultant (Various Clients)"*).
3. **Passive Task Bullet Points**: Listing job descriptions instead of achievements (e.g., *"Responsible for managing customer requests"*).
   - *Fix*: Transform to active achievement (e.g., *"Resolved 120+ tier-2 customer tickets weekly, maintaining 98% CSAT score"*).
4. **Vague Growth Claims Without Metrics**: Claims like *"significantly increased sales"* without numerical backing.
   - *Fix*: Provide baseline and final figures (e.g., *"Increased regional ARR by 34% ($1.2M to $1.6M) within 2 quarters"*).
5. **Overused Buzzwords & Fluff**: Passive filler terms (`guru`, `ninja`, `hardworking`, `synergistic`).
   - *Fix*: Replace with verified technical and business tools.
6. **Inconsistent Employment Dates**: Mismatched date formats or overlapping full-time roles creating confusion.
7. **Typographical & Grammar Errors**: Signals lack of detail orientation.
8. **Excessive Page Length**: 3+ pages for candidates with under 10 years of experience.
