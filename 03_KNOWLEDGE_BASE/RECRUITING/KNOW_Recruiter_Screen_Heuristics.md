# KNOW: Recruiter 6-Second Screen Heuristics

## Purpose
Explains eye-tracking patterns, cognitive biases, and visual hierarchy rules utilized by human recruiters during initial resume screening.

---

## The 6-Second Eye-Tracking F-Pattern

Recruiters do not read resumes word-for-word on initial screening. They skim following a top-down **F-Pattern**:

```
[Top Header: Name, Location, Target Title] ───────────────► Primary Anchor (1 sec)
                     │
[Professional Summary: Top 2 Lines] ─────────────────────► Position Verification (1 sec)
                     │
[Most Recent Job Title & Company Name] ──────────────────► Level & Prestige Check (2 sec)
                     │
[Recent Experience: Lead Action Verbs & Numbers] ────────► Quick Impact Scan (2 sec)
```

---

## Key Screening Anchors

1. **Title Alignment**: Does the recent job title match or directly map to the target position?
2. **Company Tier & Prestige**: Is the previous employer recognized, or is the industry context clear?
3. **Tenure & Stability**: Are employment durations reasonable (e.g., 2+ years average per role) or fragmented?
4. **Quantified Metrics**: Do bullet points begin with active verbs and end with clear numbers (`$`, `%`, scale)?
5. **Visual Density**: Is text broken into 2-3 line bullets with sufficient white space, or is it a wall of block text?
