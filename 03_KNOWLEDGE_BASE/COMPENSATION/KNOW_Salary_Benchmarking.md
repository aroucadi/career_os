# KNOW: Salary Benchmarking & Positioning Strategy

## Purpose
Methodologies for estimating candidate market value using percentile ranges, geographical cost-of-living tiers, and level equivalencies.

---

## Percentile Positioning Strategy

- **25th Percentile (Below Band)**: Indicates under-market offer or junior candidate level.
- **50th Percentile (Midpoint Band)**: Typical target for standard internal hires meeting baseline requirements.
- **75th–90th Percentile (Top Band)**: Attainable when candidate possesses competing written offers, specialized hard skill combinations, or top-tier company prestige.

---

## Benchmark Sources & Triangulation
Guide candidates to triangulate base salary and equity bands using reliable datasets:
1. **Levels.fyi**: Technical, Product, and Sales compensation across tier-1 tech firms.
2. **Comprehensive.io**: Live US salary disclosure data from job postings.
3. **Payscale / Radford**: Enterprise HR benchmark data.
