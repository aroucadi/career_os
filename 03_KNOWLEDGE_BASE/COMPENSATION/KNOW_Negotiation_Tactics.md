# KNOW: Behavioral Negotiation Tactics & Counter-Offer Scripts

## Purpose
Behavioral psychology, leverage frameworks, and communication scripts for negotiating offer components without risking job offers.

---

## The 4 Rules of Risk-Free Negotiation

1. **Never Negotiate Without Excitement**: Always express genuine enthusiasm for the team and role *before* introducing counter-offer points.
2. **Anchor to Market & Value, Not Personal Expenses**: Frame requests around market benchmark data and business impact, never personal financial obligations.
3. **Group All Requests into One Counter**: Present all desired adjustments (salary, signing bonus, equity, remote flexibility) in a single counter-offer email to avoid back-and-forth fatigue.
4. **Use Non-Threatening Anchors**: Frame requests collaboratively (e.g., *"If we can reach $X base, I am prepared to sign immediately"*).

---

## Negotiation Script Frameworks

### Counter-Offer Script (Base Salary Adjustment)
> *"I am thrilled about the prospect of joining [Company] as [Title]! Based on my specialized background in [Skill 1] and [Skill 2], alongside recent market data for this level in [Location], I was hoping we could look at a base salary of $X. If we can reach that number, I would be delighted to accept and sign right away."*
