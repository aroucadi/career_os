# KNOW: Total Compensation Architecture

## Purpose
Explains equity vehicles, annual bonuses, base salaries, and signing bonuses across startup and enterprise environments.

---

## Total Compensation (TC) Breakdown

$$\text{Total Compensation (TC)} = \text{Base Salary} + \text{Annual Target Bonus} + \text{Annual Equity Vest} + \text{Signing Bonus}$$

---

## Equity Vehicles Comparison

| Equity Type | Target Company Type | Mechanism | Tax Implications |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **RSUs (Restricted Stock Units)** | Public / Late-Stage Tech | Liquid shares vested over time (typically 4-year vest, 1-year cliff) | Taxed as ordinary income upon vesting date. |
| **ISOs (Incentive Stock Options)** | Early/Mid-Stage Startup | Option to purchase shares at fixed strike price | Potential AMT tax impact upon exercise. |
| **NSOs (Non-Qualified Stock Options)** | Contractors / Advisory | Option to purchase shares at strike price | Taxed upon exercise as ordinary income. |
