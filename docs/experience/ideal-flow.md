# CareerOS: Ideal User Flow & Experience Blueprint

## 1. The Day-0 through Day-30 Customer Journey

### Day 0 (First Run — Anonymous Icebreaker)
- **Trigger**: Direct visit to CareerOS URL.
- **Entry State**: Clean slate. No signup modal, no mandatory authentication, zero credit card request.
- **Anchor Viewport**: High-impact Mandate Matcher hero.
  - Option A: Upload CV (Drag & drop PDF / DOCX).
  - Option B: Instant Demo Run with Mandate #34 (Lead AI & Cloud Strategist).
- **Time to First Value**: $< 5\text{ seconds}$.
- **Glance Output**: Match Score (`71%`), 1 High-Impact Gap, 1 dominant action (`[Review & Apply Diff]`).

### Day 1 (Tactical Remediation)
- **Trigger**: Candidate reviews missing competencies against target JD.
- **Interaction**: Tap interactive checkbox on grounded bullet proposal.
- **Execution**: 1-tap live recalculation (`Rescore: 71% -> 88%`).
- **Safety**: Truth Guard verification confirms all bullets are derived from parsed facts.
- **Artifact**: 1-click generation of ATS-immune A4 PDF with zero hallucinated fluff.

### Day 7 (Adversarial Stress-Testing)
- **Trigger**: Recruiter round scheduled.
- **Command**: `/evaluate JD_34`.
- **Drawer Deep-Dive**: Prosecutor vs Advocate dialectic debate.
  - *Prosecutor*: Exposes compensation floor risks, title ambiguities, recruiter trap questions.
  - *Advocate*: Arms candidate with high-leverage value levers and negotiation scripts.
- **Outcome**: Candidate enters interview unassailable.

### Day 14+ (Passive Inbound & Account Claim)
- **Trigger**: Vetted employer in the Talent Graph matches candidate skills anonymously.
- **Notification**: High-value inbound inquiry with transparent compensation (`€950/d` or `€120k/yr`).
- **Claim Prompt**: Candidate is invited to claim their anonymous session via LinkedIn or Magic Link to decrypt contact details and grant recruiter interview access.
